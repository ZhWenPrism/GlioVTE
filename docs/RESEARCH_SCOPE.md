# Research Scope

GlioVTE investigates postoperative venous thromboembolism prediction in patients undergoing surgery for glioma in collaboration with Beijing Tiantan Hospital.

## Definitions to freeze before confirmatory modeling

- index operation and eligible glioma population;
- exclusions and repeated-admission policy;
- prediction timestamp and observation window;
- postoperative VTE definition, ascertainment source, and time horizon;
- handling of prophylaxis, anticoagulation, mobility, and competing events;
- patient-level development, calibration, internal validation, and external validation splits;
- missing-data, imbalance, and censoring strategy;
- primary discrimination and calibration metrics;
- subgroup and clinical-utility analyses.

## Evidence boundary

No performance claim should be published until it resolves to a frozen cohort, feature manifest, split identity, model configuration, prediction table, and evaluation record. Development results, exploratory analyses, and independently validated findings must remain distinguishable.

## Intended output

The intended research output is an interpretable postoperative VTE risk model with transparent evaluation and clearly bounded use. It is not an autonomous clinical decision system.
